This site documents my journey in robot learning and embodied AI. I will share implementation notes, experiments, and lessons from projects involving vision-language-action models, imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and sim-to-real transfer.

My current work focuses on three questions:

1. How can robots connect natural-language instructions with visual perception and manipulation?
2. How can we learn robust policies from a limited number of demonstrations?
3. How can policies trained in simulation transfer reliably to real robots?

The goal is to keep these notes concrete and reproducible: architecture choices, failed experiments, training details, and practical engineering trade-offs.
